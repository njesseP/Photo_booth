About

LDR PhotoBooth helps long-distance couples, friends and family make shared photo strips and keepsakes. Everything runs in your browser: photos never leave your device unless you share them yourself.

Hosting & backend

This is a static site: drop it on Netlify or Vercel for free. Cloud galleries, accounts and shareable links need a backend (for example Supabase: auth + Postgres + storage with row-level security, or Firebase). Those services have free tiers with storage and bandwidth limits — check current limits before launch. Real-time remote sessions would additionally need WebRTC or a realtime channel.

Help

Camera

Press “Start camera” and allow access when your browser asks. If you blocked it, click the lock icon in the address bar and allow the camera, or use Upload. Camera needs HTTPS.

Upload

Choose photos from your gallery (images up to 20 MB). They fill empty slots in order.

Edit

Tap a photo or sticker to select it, drag to move, use the sliders to zoom and rotate. “Retake slot” clears the selected photo; Earlier/Later reorders.

Download & share

Choose PNG or JPEG and Download. “Share” uses your device’s share sheet. “Save” keeps it in Our Memories on this device.
